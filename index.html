4-DAY WEEKLY WARS

Every week, 48 Free Fire teams are divided line-wise across 4 match days (12 squads per day). Build your squad once and compete in all weekly editions!

PERMANENT SQUAD ROSTER BUILDER

FREE FIRE SQUAD REGISTRATION

Rules: Every player must create an account first. Enter their @username to fetch verified IGN & UID. Once created, your 4+1 roster is locked and can be reused for all weekly wars!

48-Slot Line Allocation

Auto Grouping

Slots are assigned strictly in chronological order of registration:

Group A (Day 1): Slots #1 to #12
Group B (Day 2): Slots #13 to #24
Group C (Day 3): Slots #25 to #36
Group D (Day 4): Slots #37 to #48

Permanent Roster Rule

  • Once 4 core players + 1 optional sub are locked, they cannot create duplicate squads.
  • Use your existing squad for 1-click registration across all future Weekly Wars.
  • Only the squad Captain receives the Day-Wise Room ID & Password.
